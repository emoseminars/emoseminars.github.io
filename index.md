Welcome to EMO (Energy, Markets, and Optimization) Talks, a biweekly webinar series bringing together researchers from operations management, operations research, and electrical engineering to advance the research of energy and sustainability. Energy and sustainability challenges are among the most consequential problems of our time, and addressing them rigorously requires methodological tools and domain knowledge that span multiple research communities.

Our goal is to foster cross-community dialogue around the newest methodological developments in market design, optimization, stochastic modeling, and network analysis, addressing problems at the frontier of energy and sustainability. By featuring a diverse mix of junior and senior researchers, EMOS Talks aim to showcase both foundational contributions and emerging directions, providing a platform where rigorous methodology meets real-world impact and where researchers in this space can build connections across disciplinary boundaries.

EMO Talks are organized by [Jerry Anunrojwong](https://jerryanunroj.github.io), [Cheng Guo](https://chengg04.github.io), and [Junjie Qin](https://engineering.purdue.edu/people/junjie.qin.1). 

**Time**: 3:00 pm RT, biwekly on Wednesdays
