Welcome to EMO (Energy, Markets, and Optimization) Talks, a biweekly webinar series bringing together energy and sustainability researchers from Operations Management, Operations Research, and Electrical Engineering. 

Addressing emerging challenges in energy and sustainability requires methodological tools and domain knowledge that span multiple research communities. EMO Talks provides a cross-community platform where rigorous methodology meets real-world impact, spotlighting advances in relevant methodologies such as market design, optimization, stochastic modeling, and network analysis, as well as innovative ways in using them for energy and sustainability problems. 

EMO Talks are organized by [Jerry Anunrojwong](https://jerryanunroj.github.io), [Cheng Guo](https://chengg04.github.io), and [Junjie Qin](https://engineering.purdue.edu/people/junjie.qin.1). 

**Time**: 3:00 PM ET, biweekly on Wednesdays
